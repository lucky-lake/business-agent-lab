## 2. Findings

1. **autumn-sale: fewer clicks and fewer purchases at the same spend.** Spend stayed at $780 and impressions barely moved (52,000 → 51,000), but clicks fell from 1,300 to 1,020 and purchases from 39 to 25. Both the click step (CTR 2.50% → 2.00%) and the purchase step (conversion 3.00% → 2.45%) dropped. Revenue per purchase stayed at $70, so order value did not change. Cost per purchase rose from $20.00 to $31.20 and ROAS fell from 3.50 to 2.24.
   - Did the sale end, or did the sale discount get smaller, while the ads kept running?
   - Did the ad creative or offer text change this week?
   - Did the landing page, sale prices, or stock of sale items change?
   - Are people who already saw the ad several times now seeing it again (ad fatigue)?

The other three campaigns changed little. retargeting moved most of the three, with cost per purchase down 3.7% and ROAS up 3.8%. new-arrivals and brand-search stayed within about 2% on cost per purchase and ROAS.
