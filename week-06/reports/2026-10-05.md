## 2. Findings

1. **new-arrivals: CTR halved, cost per purchase more than doubled.** Impressions rose (30,000 → 32,000) and spend rose slightly ($540 → $560), but clicks fell from 900 to 480 and purchases from 18 to 8. Cost per purchase went from $30 to $70 and ROAS from 2.33 to 1.00. Revenue per purchase stayed at $70, so the drop is in clicks and conversions, not order value.
   - Did the ad creative, headline, or image change in this week?
   - Did targeting or placements change, so the ads reached a less interested audience?
   - Were the products shown in the ads out of stock, or did the landing page change?
   - Is click tracking for this campaign working the same way as the week before?

2. **retargeting: ROAS up from 3.00 to 4.00 at the same spend.** Spend stayed at $600 and CTR stayed at 4.00%, but purchases rose from 20 to 24 and revenue per purchase rose from $90 to $100.
   - Did the retargeting audience grow or change, for example with visitors who clicked new-arrivals ads in earlier weeks but did not buy?
   - Did a price change, bundle, or offer raise the order value for these buyers?
   - Did the attribution window or attribution model change, so more purchases are credited to retargeting?

autumn-sale and brand-search stayed within about 1% on all four measures.
